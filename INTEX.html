03 / SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

From sensor to decision.

The same core monitoring workflow can be adapted to different environments, with appropriate hardware and operating limits.

STEP 01

COLLECT

Suitable wearable and environmental sensors gather measurements.

STEP 02

PROCESS

A controller checks signal quality and compares readings with limits.

STEP 03

DETECT

Configured rules flag unusual measurements or missing sensor data.

STEP 04

RESPOND

The dashboard presents alerts for review and appropriate action.

DEPLOYMENT / EARTH

Field operations

Potential applications include remote expeditions, cold-weather fieldwork, emergency-response training and isolated work sites.

DEPLOYMENT / SPACE

Space missions

Could support crew awareness in spacecraft or simulated space habitats, subject to strict hardware and mission requirements.

DESIGN / ADAPTATION

Environment-specific modules

Sensor selection, communication methods, power management and alert thresholds must be adapted to each deployment.
